Acrilic's Master Integration Engine is a structured, practical handbook designed to transform integration from a trial-and-error guessing game into a systematic, logical process. It lays out a step-by-step roadmap—beginning with essential cleanup operations, mapping out core integration base cases, and providing a deterministic decision engine for selecting advanced techniques.

### Core Sections Covered

* **Phase Zero: The "Clean Up":** Techniques for algebraic expansion, fraction splitting, and trigonometric reduction to simplify integrands before applying complex methods.
* **Choosing Your Path (The Decision Engine):** A tactical selection guide covering substitution, integration by parts (using the ALPES order), partial fraction decomposition, and trigonometric substitution.
* **The Riemann Integral & Fundamental Theorems:** Rigorous foundations of definite integrals and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
* **Improper Integrals:** Criteria for identifying and analyzing Species I (infinite intervals) and Species II (infinite discontinuities) improper integrals.
* **Introduction to Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs):** Fundamental methods for solving separable and first-order linear ODEs, including real-world applications like Newton's Law of Cooling.
* **Geometric Applications:** Calculating exact areas under curves and bounded between intersecting functions.
